# HR & Behavioral Interview Preparation Knowledge

## STAR Method
- **Situation**: Describe background scenario clearly.
- **Task**: Explain specific responsibility or challenge.
- **Action**: Outline specific steps taken using "I" statements.
- **Result**: Highlight quantifiable outcomes, lessons learned, or positive results.

## Key Areas
1. **Self-Introduction**: Concise summary covering academic background, core projects, key strengths, and career passion.
2. **Strengths & Weaknesses**: Highlight real technical abilities with examples; discuss weaknesses alongside concrete mitigation strategies.
3. **Conflict Resolution**: Active listening, objective communication, focus on team goals rather than personal disagreement.
4. **Career Vision**: 3-5 year goal emphasizing skill growth, technical contributions, and leadership potential.
